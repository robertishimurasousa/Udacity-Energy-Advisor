Seasonal energy management at home

Hot weather and heat waves
Before a forecast heat wave, check that cooling is working and clear the supply vents. Close shades on windows that get direct sun during the hottest part of the day. Use ceiling fans in occupied rooms and turn them off when people leave. If the house will be occupied during extreme heat, choose a cooling schedule that maintains safe indoor conditions.

Cold weather and cold snaps
Check doors and windows for drafts before the first cold spell. Use suitable weatherstripping on movable parts or caulk small stationary gaps. Open coverings on windows that receive useful winter sun during the day and close them after dark to reduce heat loss. Prepare the heating system before a forecast cold snap and choose settings appropriate for the household and the equipment.

Adjusting schedules between seasons
Use the local forecast to get ready for the next few days and the home's energy history to compare similar weather. Review appliance timers when the utility changes its seasonal rate schedule. For a home with rooftop solar, review which hours historically have enough seasonal generation for flexible loads. Measure savings by comparing kWh and cost under similar conditions.
